Speedjacker — Account and data deletion

Android package com.aditybdev.speedjack · Published by Aditya Bhat

Delete through the app

In the app's Privacy screen, use Delete saved drives for local drive history, Delete pending feedback for unsent records, or Delete submitted feedback for service records linked to this installation. Optional account controls are separate. The email steps below provide a request path without reinstalling the app.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email dev.adityb@gmail.com with the subject “Speedjacker data deletion”.
  2. State whether you want account deletion, removal of selected connected data, or support correspondence removed. Include the account email if applicable. For an anonymous installation, include its app identity only if available.
  3. The developer may need to verify ownership and identify the records covered by your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private files or payment-card details.

What the request covers

Requests can cover optional account records, submitted camera feedback, support requests and diagnostics associated with the app installation. GPS fixes and saved route traces stay on your device; the service does not receive your raw GPS trail. Online maps and providers process their own connection information.

Retained records and timing

Submissions and private review decisions are scheduled for deletion after 90 days; automatic removal can take additional processing time. Quota and deletion controls remain for seven days. Independently reviewed map corrections and store/provider records are separate.

Account deletion does not itself cancel a store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your app-store account. Remove exported files, device backups and copies held by other recipients separately.
