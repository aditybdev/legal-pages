Become an Ancestor — Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Android package dev.adityb.becomeanancestor

Published by Aditya Bhat through MarathonApps. This notice explains app data, connected services and your controls.

Local archive

Memory text and detail records are stored in an encrypted local SQLite vault. The vault key and profile settings use operating-system secure storage. Microphone, camera and selected-media access are used when you choose to capture or import memories. Archive content can include personal and family information.

Optional connected services

Connected accounts, invitations, sharing and sync can involve identity, selected archive content, encrypted records and media pointers. Firebase provides configured authentication and sync services. Optional cloud AI requires configuring a provider and consenting to an operation. A selected provider can receive the content you send for that operation and process service records under its own policies. The local vault is distinct from connected account data.

AI and exports

Supported local AI models can run on the device. Human-readable exports can contain private memories, media and identity information. Protect exported files and choose recipients carefully. A shared or exported copy should not be assumed to remain encrypted. Biometric unlock uses device authentication; the app does not receive your biometric template.

Retention and deletion

Local memories remain until you remove them or clear app data. Local vault controls support export and memory deletion. Remove exported files and copies on other devices separately. Connected account deletion is separate from removal of each local vault. Email the contact below to request removal of connected account data or support correspondence; do not send a password or vault key.

Advertising and payments

The default local app does not display advertising. Connected services can process technical connection and account information. Native paid access is currently unconfigured; a web purchase does not establish a native-app entitlement.

Contact and support

Email dev.adityb@gmail.com for support, privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Include the app name and account email if applicable. Do not send passwords, vault keys or sensitive files. Email correspondence includes your address and any content you choose to send. Connected providers follow their own privacy and retention practices.
