Intirear — Account and data deletion

Android package com.intirear.app · Published by Shadaya Exim (OPC) Private Limited, trading as Intirear

Delete through the app

Use Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account, or follow the email steps below without reinstalling the app. The app also provides Export My Data.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email privacy@intirear.design with the subject “Intirear data deletion”.
  2. State whether you want account deletion, removal of selected connected data, or support correspondence removed. Include the account email if applicable. For an anonymous installation, include its app identity only if available.
  3. The developer may need to verify ownership and identify the records covered by your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private files or payment-card details.

What the request covers

Requests can cover account and profile information, account-linked projects, room images, prompts, design history and support records. Files exported or shared outside your account must be managed separately.

Retained records and timing

The current policy provides removal or anonymization within a commercially reasonable timeframe, subject to legal, billing, security and fraud-prevention retention requirements. Store and provider records follow their own requirements.

Account deletion does not itself cancel a store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your app-store account. Remove exported files, device backups and copies held by other recipients separately.
