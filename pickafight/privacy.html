Pick a Fight — Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Android package games.marathon.pickafight

Published by Aditya Bhat through MarathonApps. This notice explains app data, connected services and your controls.

Scope

This notice covers the native Android and iOS editions. YouTube and Roblox editions use their own platform services and are distinct from the native app.

Local game data

Campaign progress, equipment, scores, settings and the fictional game-world checkpoint are saved on your device through native preferences. A game-world checkpoint is not device location. The developer does not receive these local saves. Operating-system backups can include app data according to device settings.

Permissions and services

The native edition has no player registration, advertisements, in-app purchases, developer analytics, chat or developer-hosted save server. It does not request camera, contacts, microphone or device-location access. Speech uses an available device voice; the game does not record you. App stores and operating-system services may process information under their own policies.

Removal

Starting a new odyssey resets campaign progress while retaining equipment, settings and the best score. Clear app storage to remove all local game data. Device backups must be managed separately.

Audience

The native adult edition contains strong fictional language and fantasy combat and displays an 18+ confirmation. It is intended for adults.

Contact and support

Email dev.adityb@gmail.com for support, privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Include the app name and account email if applicable. Do not send passwords, vault keys or sensitive files. Email correspondence includes your address and any content you choose to send. Connected providers follow their own privacy and retention practices.
