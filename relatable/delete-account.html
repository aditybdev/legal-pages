Relatable — Account and data deletion

Android package app.relatable.app · Published by Relatable

Delete through the app

Request account deletion in the app's Settings or through the Parent Dashboard for a child profile. You can also use the email steps below without reinstalling the app.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email privacy@relatable.study with the subject “Relatable data deletion”.
  2. State whether you want account deletion, removal of selected connected data, or support correspondence removed. Include the account email if applicable. For an anonymous installation, include its app identity only if available.
  3. The developer may need to verify ownership and identify the records covered by your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private files or payment-card details.

What the request covers

Your account data and associated child profiles, account-linked conversations, learning records, notes and uploaded files are covered by a request. Public shares can be revoked. Copies other people have saved or forked are separate from your account.

Retained records and timing

Active systems, backups and provider records can follow different removal processes. The privacy policy does not promise one fixed erasure period for all records. Records required by law, including payment or tax records, may be retained.

Account deletion does not itself cancel a store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your app-store account. Remove exported files, device backups and copies held by other recipients separately.
