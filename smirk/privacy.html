Smirk — Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Android package com.smirknow.app

Published by Aditya Bhat through MarathonApps. This notice explains app data, connected services and your controls.

Scope and accounts

Smirk was previously developed under the Nano Banana project name. Configured Firebase Authentication processes account email, user ID and profile information. Google or Apple sign-in can supply email and profile information according to your sign-in choices. Configured Firebase services store jobs and favorites. Settings, recent work and debugging events can also be stored on your device.

Photos, prompts and AI

When you submit a creation job, selected images, prompts, model choices and generation controls can be sent to the configured generation gateway and AI provider. Kie.ai is the implemented direct provider; a configured gateway can route a chosen job to other displayed providers. Providers receive input needed to generate or edit content and process service records under their own policies. Do not submit private material about other people without permission. Camera and selected-media permissions support capture and import flows you choose.

Credits and purchases

Google Play or the App Store processes native purchases. Adapty supports credit products, purchase history and entitlement checks. The developer does not receive full payment-card details. Purchase and service records can include device identifiers and technical information needed for billing, security and service operation.

Analytics and diagnostics

App events are retained locally for debugging when analytics consent is absent. Optional forwarding depends on both consent and a configured analytics provider. Connected services can process connection logs, installation identifiers, device/app information, job errors and purchase diagnostics. Local debugging records are distinct from remote analytics. The app does not currently display advertisements.

Retention and deletion

Manage consent and account deletion through the app controls. You can also email the contact below with 'Smirk account deletion' and your account email. Ownership may need to be verified; do not send a password. Account data and jobs are separate from local files, exports, app-store records and provider service logs. Remove local/exported copies separately and use provider privacy controls where needed. Records required for billing, security or legal obligations may be retained.

Contact and support

Email dev.adityb@gmail.com for support, privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Include the app name and account email if applicable. Do not send passwords, vault keys or sensitive files. Email correspondence includes your address and any content you choose to send. Connected providers follow their own privacy and retention practices.
