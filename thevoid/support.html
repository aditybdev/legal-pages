Widgets are refreshed by the operating system on a schedule that The Void does not control. To force a refresh on iOS, open The Void briefly; the widget will sync on next refresh. Make sure "Background App Refresh" is enabled in iPhone Settings > The Void. On Android, ensure the app is not being restricted by battery optimization. Go to Settings > Apps > The Void > Battery > Unrestricted.

Party Mode requires microphone and speech recognition permissions. Check the following:

iOS: Go to iPhone Settings > The Void and ensure both Microphone and Speech Recognition are enabled.

Android: Go to Settings > Apps > The Void > Permissions and enable Microphone. If voice still does not work, ensure your device language is supported by the on-device speech engine.

Party Mode processes all voice data locally on your device. No audio is sent to any server.

The Void watch companion communicates with your phone using the platform's native watch connectivity framework.

Apple Watch: Ensure The Void is installed on your Apple Watch via the Watch app on your iPhone. If it does not appear, open the Watch app > My Watch > scroll to The Void > Install. If sync fails, try restarting both devices.

Galaxy Watch: Install The Void companion from the Galaxy Store on your watch. Ensure Bluetooth is enabled and both devices are paired. If the watch shows stale data, open The Void on your phone to trigger a sync.

Void Roulette is a party game for 2 to 8 players. Each round, a player asks a question and The Void delivers an answer. Players vote on the best question-answer pairing, and points are awarded. The game continues for a set number of rounds, and the player with the most points is crowned the winner. Void Roulette is free for everyone.

The Daily Void delivers one new answer every day, available to everyone. It appears on your home screen widget and within the app. Think of it as your daily fortune, delivered by an entity that does not care about your feelings. The Daily Void refreshes at midnight in your local time zone.
