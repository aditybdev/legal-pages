The Void support

Updated 16 October 2026 · Marathon Apps

Ask a question, choose an intent and make a little room for possibility.

How do I ask another question?

After the answer, tap Ask again or the back arrow. Home clears the previous question and focuses the input. You can also swipe down to dismiss the answer.

Which features are free?

Core readings, the daily message, History and starter games are free. Optional answer packs, a portal theme, occasion decks and the personal-deck creator are one-time App Store purchases. There is no subscription or advertising.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that bought the extra. Open Settings → Extras → Restore purchases with an internet connection. Verified ownership is cached for offline use. Apple handles billing and refunds; the developer cannot access your payment card.

Which languages are supported?

Choose Follow phone or English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese or German in Settings → Language. Menus and bundled answers use the chosen language. Your own questions, player names and personal deck text are preserved.

Does it work offline?

Core readings, saved History, games and the public daily message work on your device. Optional AI runs on supported iPhones with Apple Intelligence; otherwise the bundled library is used. Purchases, sharing destinations and system dictation may need a connection.

How do I use dictation?

Tap the microphone beside the question and allow microphone and speech recognition access. Speak, then stop listening. If no words were understood, try again or type instead. Enable permissions in iPhone Settings if you declined them. Your operating system may process speech online.

How does the daily widget work?

Add The Void widget from the Home Screen widget gallery. It includes its own public daily library and updates at local midnight subject to the system's scheduling. On iOS 17 and later, touch and hold it, choose Edit Widget and select a language. It never shows your private questions.

How do reminders and streaks work?

Enable a reminder and choose a time in Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally. A completed reading counts once for the local calendar day; canceled preparation does not count. Streak rewards are earned through continued use.

Can I save, share or make my own deck?

Completed readings are in History, where you can search and mark favorites. Share creates an image on your device and opens the system share sheet. Playroom includes challenge codes and deck previews. The personal-deck creator has a limited trial and an optional one-time unlock. Exported codes and decks are readable by recipients.

Where is my data?

History, favorites, preferences, games, personal decks and optional session counts are local. We run no account or question server. Device backups may contain app data. Settings → Delete All Data clears local data and cancels reminders; it cannot erase shared copies, backups or Apple purchase records.

What if a screen is stuck?

Try Back or Cancel, then reopen the app. Check free device storage if saved data cannot be read. Please email the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and steps that caused the issue. Include a screenshot only if it contains no information you wish to keep private.

What are the adult modes?

Adult modes require confirming you are at least 18. Respect every player's boundaries. Obtain consent before recording or sharing a reaction. You can skip a round or leave the game.

Purchase or refund questions can also be handled through Apple billing support.
