Privacy & usage

Updated 16 October 2026 · Marathon Apps

Privacy

On your device

Questions, history, favorites, preferences, games and personal decks are saved locally. We operate no account system, cloud database or question server. Your operating system's backups may include app data.

Speech and AI

Dictation uses your system's speech service; Apple or Google may process audio online. Optional AI answers use Apple's on-device model on supported iPhones. Otherwise, answers come from the bundled library.

Sharing and local stats

No advertising or external analytics service runs in the app. Optional session counts stay local. Images, videos, challenge codes and deck files are shared only when you choose. Shared codes are readable, not encrypted. Widgets and reminders contain no private questions.

Purchases

Apple processes optional one-time purchases and keeps its purchase records. The app caches verified product ownership for offline use. We do not receive your payment card details. Restore purchases with the same Apple Account.

Deleting your data

Delete All Data in Settings clears local history, preferences, games, decks and stats, and cancels reminders. It cannot remove copies you saved or shared elsewhere, operating-system backups, or Apple's purchase records. Temporary reaction videos are removed when you leave their screen.

Contact

For questions about your data, contact Marathon Apps at dev.adityb@gmail.com. We keep no server copy of your question history and cannot recover it.

Usage & license

For fun and reflection

The Void is entertainment, not a prediction or professional advice. Answers are playful prompts, not facts. Use your own judgment for important decisions.

Free core, optional extras

Core readings and starter games are free. Optional packs, a theme and the personal-deck creator use one-time App Store purchases. Prices appear before purchase. No subscription is offered. Restore with the same Apple Account.

Consent and adult modes

Adult modes require confirmation that you are 18 or older. Respect other players' boundaries and ask permission before recording or sharing their reactions.

Your shared content

You choose what to put in personal decks and what to share. Share only content you have permission to use. Challenge codes and exported files may reveal their questions, answers and player names to recipients.
