Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026
The Void is built on a simple principle: your data is yours. We don't want it, we don't collect it, and we don't sell it. The Void is completely free — no accounts, no purchases, no subscriptions — and it is not built on your personal information.

1. Data Minimization

The Void is designed with data minimization as a core architectural principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal usage data linked to your identity. All questions you type or speak into The Void are processed locally on your device.

We do not maintain user profiles, behavioral analytics, or cross-device tracking of any kind.

2. Local Storage

The following data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Your question and answer history
  • Favorite / saved answers
  • App settings and preferences
  • Streak and achievement progress
  • Portal skin selections

This data is stored using your device's secure local storage mechanisms. Deleting The Void from your device permanently deletes all of this data. We have no backup copy and cannot recover it.

3. Third-Party Services

The Void uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and shares no data with data processors. Because The Void has no accounts, no purchases, and no cloud sync, there is simply nothing to send. The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, whose own store policies govern the download itself.

4. Voice Processing

Party Mode uses your device's on-board speech recognition capabilities. Voice data is processed transiently, on-device, using the native speech framework provided by your operating system (Apple Speech or Android Speech Services). The Void does not record, store, or transmit audio data to any server.

5. Camera

Reaction videos use your device's camera. Capture happens entirely on your device: The Void does not upload, store, or transmit photos or video to any server.

6. Watch Companions

The Apple Watch and Galaxy Watch companion apps operate independently on the wearable device. Communication between your phone and watch uses the platform's secure device-to-device protocol. No data is routed through external servers.

7. Children's Privacy

The Void does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation, and we have no mechanism to identify the age of our users.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of The Void after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at privacy@thevoid.inc.
