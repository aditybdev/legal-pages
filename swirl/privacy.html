Swirl! — Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Android package com.icarusgames.swirl

Published by Aditya Bhat through MarathonApps. This notice explains app data, connected services and your controls.

Local game data

Progress, settings and counters are saved locally through native preferences. Local play counters retain up to ninety calendar days and are not automatically uploaded to a developer analytics server. Operating-system backups can include app data according to device settings.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and regional consent. Ad requests are configured as nonpersonalized. Nonpersonalized ads still involve collection: Google can process IP address and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, crashes and other performance/security information. Google and advertising partners have their own privacy and retention policies. Use the game's privacy choices when available and your device advertising controls to manage consent and identifiers.

Purchases

An optional native-store purchase removes automatic ad breaks. Google Play or the App Store processes payment; the app checks entitlement status and acknowledges completed Android purchases. The developer does not receive payment-card details or operate a separate purchase backend. Store records follow the store privacy policy.

Accounts and support

The game does not require a developer game account and has no developer-hosted progress server. Support email can contain an email address and whatever you choose to send. Support correspondence is retained while resolving a request and up to twelve months afterwards; request removal through the contact below.

Reset and removal

Use the game reset control or clear app storage to remove progress and local counters. Reset does not erase Google advertising logs or app-store purchase records. Manage provider records through their privacy controls.

Providers

Google explains advertising data practices at policies.google.com/privacy and developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Contact and support

Email dev.adityb@gmail.com for support, privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Include the app name and account email if applicable. Do not send passwords, vault keys or sensitive files. Email correspondence includes your address and any content you choose to send. Connected providers follow their own privacy and retention practices.
