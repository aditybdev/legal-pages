TidyUp — Account and data deletion

Android package com.swipeaway.tidy · Published by Aditya Bhat

Delete through the app

Use Settings → Privacy → Delete my data to remove the app data covered by that control. TidyUp uses anonymous app and purchase identities rather than a personal sign-in account. You can also request connected-data deletion using the email steps below.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email dev.adityb@gmail.com with the subject “TidyUp data deletion”.
  2. State whether you want account deletion, removal of selected connected data, or support correspondence removed. Include the account email if applicable. For an anonymous installation, include its app identity only if available.
  3. The developer may need to verify ownership and identify the records covered by your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private files or payment-card details.

What the request covers

Requests can cover app identity, referral and consent records associated with your installation, and support correspondence. Your photo library is on your device; TidyUp does not receive its photo or video content. Manage exported copies and device backups separately.

Retained records and timing

Advertising, analytics, crash and store providers have separate retention controls. Purchase, fraud-prevention and legally required records may need to be retained. The developer can explain the scope and status of your request.

Account deletion does not itself cancel a store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your app-store account. Remove exported files, device backups and copies held by other recipients separately.
