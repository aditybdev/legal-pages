Languar — Account and data deletion

Android package com.marathonapps.linguapersona · Published by Aditya Bhat

Delete through the app

Delete the online Firebase identity in Settings → Optional account, or use the existing account deletion page at https://languar-support.vercel.app/delete-account. Local learning deletion is separate. The email steps below provide another request path.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email dev.adityb@gmail.com with the subject “Languar data deletion”.
  2. State whether you want account deletion, removal of selected connected data, or support correspondence removed. Include the account email if applicable. For an anonymous installation, include its app identity only if available.
  3. The developer may need to verify ownership and identify the records covered by your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private files or payment-card details.

What the request covers

Requests can cover the optional online identity and identifiable support records. Phrasebook and selected learning memories are stored on your device and are not uploaded for synchronization. Delete those with the local learning controls. Content you deliberately send to the cloud tutor or reporting service is a separate service operation.

Retained records and timing

Submitted reports have a 14-day expiry and are removed through the provider's deletion process, which can take additional time. Anonymous aggregate diagnostics cannot be reliably matched back to one person. Store and Adapty billing records follow their own requirements.

Account deletion does not itself cancel a store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your app-store account. Remove exported files, device backups and copies held by other recipients separately.
