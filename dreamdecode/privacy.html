Privacy

Privacy Policy

Updated January 28, 2026

1. Privacy in plain terms

Dream Decode is built for private reflection. Your dreams are stored on your device, and we cannot read them.

2. On-device analysis

Dream analysis runs entirely on your device using a local model. Your dream text is never sent to our servers to be analyzed.

  • Dreams are encrypted at rest on your device.
  • No copy of your dream content leaves your device unless you turn on encrypted backup.

3. What we collect

Only if you create an account

  • Email address, used to sign you in.
  • Display name, which is optional.
  • Subscription status, so your plan works across devices.

Technical data

  • Basic device info and whether the local model finished setting up.
  • Anonymous performance and crash data to help fix problems.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information, and we never share your dream content with advertisers or third parties.

5. Your choices

  • View everything stored about you in the Privacy screen.
  • Permanently delete all your data from the app at any time.
  • Export your journal to a file you control.

6. Contact us

Privacy questions? Email privacy@dreamdecode.app
