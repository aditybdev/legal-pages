Dream Decode — Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Android package com.dreamdecodenow.app

Published by Aditya Bhat through MarathonApps. This notice explains app data, connected services and your controls.

Journal and processing

The app is designed for a local dream journal and supported on-device model interpretation. Journal entries and recordings can be sensitive. Microphone access is used for voice entry; selected photos and files can be imported when you choose those features. Device speech services may process speech according to their settings; speech recognition is not necessarily offline on every device.

Accounts and technical data

Account services process an email address, user ID and optional profile name. Configured Firebase services provide authentication and crash reporting. Technical information can include app/device versions, installation identifiers, crashes and performance information. Subscription services process purchase history and entitlement status. These records are distinct from the journal and are not necessarily anonymous.

Backup and sharing

Optional cloud backup encrypts journal content before upload according to the app's backup implementation. Protect your recovery material. Files you export and content you intentionally share can be readable by recipients. Cloud storage and authentication services process service data under their own policies.

Optional health integration

With your permission, Android Health Connect can supply sleep, heart-rate and step records for sleep-related context and insights. Access is optional and can be revoked in Health Connect or device settings. Health data is not used to display advertisements. Dream Decode is a reflection and journaling app, not a medical device or a source of diagnosis, treatment or emergency care.

Purchases

Google Play or the App Store processes payments. Adapty supports subscription and entitlement status. The developer does not receive full payment-card details. The app does not currently display advertisements.

Retention and controls

Use Privacy and account settings to inspect, export or delete app data. Local data, exported files, cloud account data and device backups must be managed separately. Request account or connected-data deletion through the contact below. Billing and security records may need to be retained where required.

Contact and support

Email dev.adityb@gmail.com for support, privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Include the app name and account email if applicable. Do not send passwords, vault keys or sensitive files. Email correspondence includes your address and any content you choose to send. Connected providers follow their own privacy and retention practices.
