Waterbaby — Account and data deletion

Android package com.marathonapps.waterbaby · Published by Aditya Bhat

Delete through the app

Use the account deletion control in Settings. The partner connection ends immediately and account removal is scheduled after a 30-day recovery window. You can also request deletion by email without reinstalling the app.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email dev.adityb@gmail.com with the subject “Waterbaby data deletion”.
  2. State whether you want account deletion, removal of selected connected data, or support correspondence removed. Include the account email if applicable. For an anonymous installation, include its app identity only if available.
  3. The developer may need to verify ownership and identify the records covered by your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private files or payment-card details.

What the request covers

Requests can cover account, connection, reminder, water-sharing, enabled cloud-history and support records. Private water and reminder history is normally held in account-scoped encrypted storage on the device. Manage local files and copies separately.

Retained records and timing

The app provides a 30-day account recovery window. Security, reward-eligibility, billing and legally required records may be retained as described in the current privacy policy. A request does not automatically erase records controlled by the app store or other providers.

Account deletion does not itself cancel a store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your app-store account. Remove exported files, device backups and copies held by other recipients separately.
